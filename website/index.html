How it works

Three steps. Zero servers. Your data stays yours.

  1. 1

    Connect Gmail

    Read-only access through Google's official API. Livo can never send, modify, or delete your mail.

  2. 2

    AI finds what matters

    Each email is analyzed against one question: what does this person need to know or do before it becomes a problem? Noise is aggressively filtered out.

  3. 3

    Act in one click

    A daily digest of Urgent / Needs attention / Upcoming, each with why it was flagged and what to do — cancel, remind, review.
